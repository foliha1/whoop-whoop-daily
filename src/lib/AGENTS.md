# Skins and Daily #100 (src/lib)
- Skins remap card art at render time via skinArt; card data and network payloads always carry classic art. The Spook palette shift is frozen at page load: SPOOK_AT_LOAD (src/lib/skin.ts) drives RAW in tokens.ts and data-skin="spook" on <html> (set by initSkin next to initTheme), so colors never change mid-game; text/icons on blue and red fills use COLORS.onBlue/onRed, blue/red text on the page uses COLORS.blueText/redText. Card-face recolouring is independent of UI fill colours to preserve colour-blind distinction.
- Seasonal lobby artwork and bylines use SPOOK_AT_LOAD with theme-resolved static logos capped to the existing lockup slot; the Daily share image swaps its header logo the same way, fitting the squarer Spook mark to the slot's height without stretching; seat accents live in RAW tokens, keeping skin-off presentation unchanged.
- Non-board card renderers subscribe with useSkin and remap only displayed/preloaded URLs through skinArt; card-colour copy uses colorWord with SPOOK_AT_LOAD so words match the frozen palette. Image readiness keys use JSON encoding rather than comma splitting because skin face data URLs contain commas.
- Holo Day (Daily #100) is a CSS-only skin: src/lib/holoSkin.ts gates it to 18 Nov 2026 in the player's local time (so it matches #100 everywhere), stores the choice under its own key (ww-skin-holo) and freezes HOLO_AT_LOAD at boot; src/styles/holo.css paints everything under html[data-skin="holo"]. Brand fills are matched by their serialized inline colour and only gain background-image / shadow layers, so palette tokens, card art and payloads stay classic. Card foil is loud only on the cream border (masked) and blended with overlay on the face, so red/blue/orange stay distinct and black shapes stay black during memorising. The animated logo's cream paths take #hoFoilPaint from a hidden SVG in index.html. ?skin=holo previews it any day.
- Daily #100 extras live in src/lib/daily100.ts: dailyTitle() renames #100 "100 Days of Daily" on the Home tile, start dialog and results heading; the keepsake badge (key daily-100) is derived client-side from a stored ww_daily_whoop-2026-11-18 result and merged into the badge shelf by withKeepsake, so it needs no RPC or migration; Holo100Splash (root-mounted next to SpookGhost) flips nine cards to DAY / 100 / !!! once per device under HOLO_AT_LOAD, skippable, ?splash=1 replays it.
- Spook decorations (ghost, cobwebs) render only under [data-skin="spook"]: the root-mounted SpookGhost pauses via setSpookGhostPaused during Daily runs (STUDY→WHOOPED) and live games (phase ≠ GAME_OVER), fading out promptly where it is — it must never float over cards being memorised. Cobwebs are mask-image pseudo-elements with isolation + z-index:-1 so they stay behind content; panels get overflow:hidden only under the skin.
